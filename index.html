01 — About

Engineering with purpose

A quick look at who I am, how I work, and the kind of problems I gravitate toward.

I'm Sunny Kumar — a Computer Science & Engineering undergraduate who fell in love with the moment an idea becomes a running system.

My work lives at the intersection of backend engineering and platform engineering. I write Python services with Flask and SQLAlchemy, package them with Docker, run them on Kubernetes with Helm charts, provision the cloud with Terraform, and instrument everything with Prometheus and Grafana. I care about the unglamorous parts: CSRF protection, rate limiting, secret rotation, test coverage and CI that fails loudly.

Three internships shaped that approach — CodeAlpha (cloud computing), Prodigy Infotech (artificial intelligence) and CodSoft (Python development). Between them they taught me that shipping matters more than theorising, and that a project is only finished when it can be run by someone else.

A production-grade security platform that detects SQL injection attacks, encrypts sensitive records and monitors every security event. Scored detection engine, envelope encryption and constant-time capability verification.
